Dados e IA Studio

Privacy Policy

Effective October 7, 2026. Controller: Rafael Tadeu, an individual creator in Brazil.

Resumo em português: o app só acessa a conta do TikTok do próprio criador, guarda os tokens de acesso no computador dele, não vende nem compartilha dados e não coleta dados de espectadores. Direitos da LGPD (Lei 13.709/2018) podem ser exercidos pelo contato abaixo.

Scope

Dados e IA Studio is a personal tool that uploads videos to its operator's own TikTok account. It has no public users, no website accounts, and no audience tracking.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect data about people who watch the videos.
  • It does not sell, rent, or share data with anyone other than TikTok.
  • It does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies.

Sharing

Data is sent only to TikTok, through its official APIs, to upload and publish videos. TikTok processes it under its own privacy policy.

Security

Tokens are stored locally with restricted file permissions and are never published or committed to source control. Connections to TikTok use HTTPS.

Your choices and rights

The account owner can disconnect the app at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy, Security, Apps and services permissions; this stops all access. Under the Brazilian General Data Protection Law (LGPD), you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data.

Children

The app is not directed to children and does not knowingly process children's data.

Changes and contact

Updates will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Privacy requests can be sent to the operator through the TikTok account connected to this app.
